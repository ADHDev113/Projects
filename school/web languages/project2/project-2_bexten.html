MP (Map Printer)

About MP

Originally designed for a video game concept. He is the map giver in an metroidvania type game. He is a steam powered robot printer. He is very janky and his wifi is always broken. I also realized that I originally drew him in a way so that he would only be able to walk straight, so he cannot turn without a turntable. Once you find this npc, he can print you your map in black and white, but oh no! He is out of cyan ink, which means he can't print. You need to find him some.

ELM

About ELM

One of my D&D characters that I created for a campaign. Autognome Artillerist Artificer.
ELM is an autognome robot made for the purpose of keeping a person alive after death. He was made by an old scholarly gnome when he was close to death. However, not everything went as expected. After the transfer, ELM lost the memories of who he was before (think GLADOS from Portal) and now is trying to recover memories of his past and figure out who he is. He uses his skills of tinkering and magical crafting to change his own body to suit his needs. He has a detachable cannon arm that also houses the different modules to hold his spells.
